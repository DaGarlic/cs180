# CS180 — Introduction to Computer Vision

Project write-ups for UCB CS180, Fall 2026.

Live site: **<https://dagarlic.github.io/cs180/>**

- [Project 0 — Becoming Friends with Your Camera](proj0/) — perspective, focal
  length, and the center of projection.
- [Project 1 — Images of the Russian Empire](proj1/) — colorizing the
  Prokudin-Gorskii glass plate negatives.
- [Project 2 — Fun with Filters and Frequencies](proj2/) — convolution from
  scratch, edges, derivative of Gaussian, image sharpening, hybrid images, stacks and multiresolution blending (Parts 1.1 to 1.3 and 2.1 to 2.4).
- [Project 3 — Fun with Flow Models](proj3/) — sampling, guidance and editing with a pretrained flow model (visual anagrams, hybrid images), and flow matching from scratch on MNIST.
- [Project 4 — [Auto]Stitching Photo Mosaics](proj4/) — homographies, projective warping and blending (Part A), then Harris corners, feature matching and RANSAC for automatic stitching (Part B).
